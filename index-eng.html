PUCRA is the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya's robotics association, based at the Escola d'Enginyeria de Barcelona Est, the most innovative campus of the university. Founded in 2017, it is the first and only association of this type in the entire Spanish territory.

With a multidisciplinary technical team made up of students from different branches of engineering, we are ready to overcome any challenge that comes our way. Our culture of work, effort and sacrifice and the ambition to continue improving leads us to exceed our goals and objectives every year.
