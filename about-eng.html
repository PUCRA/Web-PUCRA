THE HISTORY OF PUCRA

COMPETITIONS

September 2017

BEGINNING OF PUCRA

The need to continue learning outside the classroom and to be able to share dreams and concerns with other students motivated the founding of PUCRA in 2017, the first and only association of this type in the entire Spanish territory. The main objective has always been to participate and win the most important international competitions in the sector.

Participations of the competition team

September 2018

ORIGIN OF PUCRA FACTORY

The project arose from the need to give continuity to the new generation of the association, encouraging the scientific-technological vocations of the youngest. Over the past few years we have tutored several of our own high school and high school teams to participate in the High School category of the VEX Robotics Competition.

February 2020

UPC ASSOCIATIONS AWARD

The PUCRA received the 1st prize aimed at UPC student associations to recognize and promote associations, their involvement and the promotion of the inspiring values ​​of university life.

September 2020

SOCIAL PLAN

Given that our campus is located between the neighborhoods of El Besòs and La Mina, as part of the social responsibility activities carried out by the management of our school, we have collaborated in order to give the opportunity to several socio-educational centers in the area to be able to participate in the IQ category of the VEX Robotics Competition, opening the horizons of more than 100 children in vulnerable situations.

November 2022

UPC Associations Award

Once again, the UPC corroborated our work and involvement in university life, granting us the association prize in the discipline of transversal studies and training, demonstrating that our project has a great vision for the future.
