PUCRA BRINGS THE BIGGEST ROBOTIC COMPETITIONS TO OUR HOME

On Saturday 3rd February PUCRA organised the regional competition at the School of Engineering of Barcelona Est, where 13 teams from Catalonia and Castelló competed, some of which were from the VEX social plan, which has benefited more than 50 children and 5 schools this year. At the end, there was a awards such as the Judges Award and the Design Award.

More Information

The weekend of March 4 and 5 took place at the Escola d'Enginyeria de Barcelona, the national final of VEX Robotics. More than 40 teams of different categories, have put their robots and skills to the test to qualify for the grand finale World Championship that took place last April in Dallas (Texas). An event open to public that has promised a weekend full of successes and emotions.

Gallery More Information

After three years being Event Partners of VEX Robotics in our country, PUCRA has once again brought the national final to the Escola dEnginyeria de Barcelona Est on February 26th and 27th. An edition that has broken participation records in all categories, and that has demonstrated the importance of educational robotics and its growth in recent years.

More Information

In an unusual year full of difficulties, PUCRA has been able to hold a new edition of the national final of the VEX Robotics Contest, with the presence of 25 teams and more than 150 competitors. Reinventing ourselves and taking advantage of the growing streaming trends, we managed to reach more than 2,000 viewers, in a first semi-in-person edition due to Covid-19 restrictions.

Gallery More Information

PUCRA has converted Barcelona, during the month of February, in the capital of robotics. For the second consecutive year it took place at the Escola d'Enginyeria de Barcelona the Barcelona's regional final of VEX Robotics. Furthermore, in the building, vertex of the North Campus of the UPC, the national final of the VEX Robotics Competition was held simultaneously with the first edition of the VEX Mediterranean Open with the participation of national and international teams.

More Information Gallery

In our second year as a competition team we have gotten involved fully in the VEX Robotics community of our country, becoming organizers of the Barcelona regional final. Being the first year as Event Partners, we have gathered, at the Escola d'Enginyeria de Barceloan Est, more than 700 people and more than 20 teams to compete, enjoy and share their passion for robotics and technology.
