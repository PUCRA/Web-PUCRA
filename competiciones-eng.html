COMPETITIONS PUCRA

THE HISTORY OF PUCRA

MARCH 2018

VEX Robotics 2018

In our first year, PUCRA participated in the VEX Robotics Competition, qualifying for the state finals. Winning the award as national finalist and first university team in the competition, a magnificent result for the team's first participation.

APRIL 2018

EUROBOT SPAIN 2018

Just two months after its victory in VEX, PUCRA participated in Eurobot 2018, where it consolidated itself in 25th place in the world ranking of more than 3,000 teams, achieving the best national classification.

SEPTEMBER 2018

FRA - ROBOT EXTREME

In preparation for the second season, the team decided to participate in a competition format different from anything before. Robot fighting is an intense, exciting and technically very demanding competition, where PUCRA stood out for its potential.

PUCRA managed to be a finalist and European champion in the Robot Extreme held in Colchester (United Kingdom) where it competed against the best teams in the world.

FEBRUARY 2019

VEX ROBOTICS 2019

After participating in the first year in VEX Robotics and the experience gained in other competitions, we managed to become national champions, qualifying for the grand final of the VEX WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP 2019, held in Louisville (Kentucky).

Fighting side by side with the best teams from the five continents, overcoming the knockout phases, PUCRA obtained the best classification as a European team in the history of the competition. An unforgettable experience that propelled the team to another level.

JULY 2019

ICE VISION HACK A.I AND M.L.

One more advance for the competition team, participating in a new discipline. ICE VISION HACK is an artificial intelligence and machine learning competition taking place in Moscow, Russia. The PUCRA managed to become European champion of the contest, reaffirming its position as a national and international robotics association.

>

SEPTEMBER 2020

EUROPEAN ROVER CHALLENGE 2020

In the third season, PUCRA decided to go one step further and face a new technological challenge. The European Rover Challenge (ERC), a university competition that aims to solve the real problems of current space missions.

Despite the months of pandemic and mixed presence at the university, the team managed to reinvent itself by designing THARSIS, an autonomous robot focused on space exploration, to participate in the ERC 2020 in online format. With more than 100 international teams, PUCRA managed to reach the ERC finals in the first year, achieving a proud eighth place.

JANUARY 2021

THARSIS 2021

After participating in the online edition of the ERC, the team focused all its efforts on the evolution and construction of THARSIS. From the mechanical design and manufacturing of all structural and engine systems, to the implementation of neural networks for autonomous navigation and detection, in addition to the in-house design of all power and control electronics.

APRIL 2022

VEX ROBOTICS 2022

In a season full of challenges, PUCRA had the opportunity to participate again in the grand final of the VEX WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP 2022, held in Dallas (Texas).

A great opportunity to meet and collaborate with other international teams, learning from their methodologies and objectives, to give a very different approach to the next season.

FEBRUARY 2023

VEX ROBOTICS 2023

This season the team has participated in a specific modality of the VEX Robotics Competition, achieving eighth position in the world autonomous skills ranking.

The team participates for the second consecutive time in the grand final of the VEX WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP 2023, held in Dallas (Texas) at the end of April.

Reinventing the future. PUCRA
