COMPETITIONS PUCRA

THE HISTORY OF PUCRA

May 2025

SENER-CEA’s Bot Talent 2025

On May 6th, the PUCRA team traveled to Madrid to participate for the first time in the “Sener-CEA’s Bot Talent” competition, a challenge focused on the design and programming of autonomous robots inspired by NASA missions. In this event, the team achieved a double victory, winning both the design award and the overall jury award. The team, made up entirely of undergraduate students, showcased strong skills in computer vision, intelligent navigation, and applied AI. This success reflects the talent, dedication, and teamwork of its members.

February 2023

VEX ROBOTICS 2023

This season, the team participated in a specific category of the VEX Robotics Competition, achieving eighth place in the global autonomous Skills ranking.

The team took part for the second consecutive year in the VEX WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP 2023 finals, held in Dallas (Texas) at the end of April.

April 2022

VEX ROBOTICS 2022

In a season full of challenges, PUCRA had the opportunity to participate again in the VEX WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP 2022 finals, held in Dallas (Texas).

A great chance to meet and collaborate with international teams, learning from their methods and goals, which offered a new perspective for the following season.

January 2021

THARSIS 2021

After participating in the online edition of the ERC, the team focused all its efforts on the development and construction of THARSIS. From mechanical design and fabrication of structural and mobility systems to the implementation of neural networks for autonomous navigation and detection, along with the in-house design of all power and control electronics.

September 2020

EUROPEAN ROVER CHALLENGE 2020

In their third season, PUCRA decided to take a step further and tackle a new technological challenge: the European Rover Challenge (ERC), a university competition addressing real-world problems in space missions.

Despite the pandemic and hybrid university format, the team reinvented itself by designing THARSIS, an autonomous robot for space exploration, to participate in the online ERC 2020. Out of over 100 international teams, PUCRA reached the ERC finals in their first year, earning an impressive eighth place.

July 2019

ICE VISION HACK A.I AND M.L.

Another step forward for the competition team, venturing into a new field. ICE VISION HACK is an artificial intelligence and Machine Learning competition held in Moscow (Russia). PUCRA emerged as the European champion, consolidating its position as a national and international robotics association.

February 2019

VEX ROBOTICS 2019

After their first-year participation in VEX Robotics and experience in other competitions, the team became national champions, qualifying for the VEX WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP 2019 finals, held in Louisville (Kentucky).

Facing off against top teams from five continents and making it through the elimination rounds, PUCRA achieved the best European ranking in the history of the competition. An unforgettable experience that elevated the team to a new level.

September 2018

FRA - ROBOT EXTREME

In preparation for the second season, the team chose to enter a new competition format. Robot fighting is an intense, thrilling, and technically demanding event in which PUCRA stood out for its potential.

PUCRA became finalist and European champion at Robot Extreme, held in Colchester (UK), competing against the world’s top teams.

April 2018

EUROBOT SPAIN 2018

Just two months after their success in VEX, PUCRA participated in Eurobot 2018, securing 25th place in the global ranking among over 3000 teams, achieving the best national result.

March 2018

VEX Robotics 2018

In our first year, PUCRA participated in the VEX Robotics Competition, qualifying for the state finals. They were awarded as national finalists and became the first university team in the competition — an outstanding result for the team’s debut.

Reinventing the future. PUCRA
